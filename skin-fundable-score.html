Traction Signal

The proof point that converts conviction to capital. Weight increases dramatically with stage — at Series A, this dimension overrides almost everything else.

Weight: 15% at pre-seed
Tier 1 — Deal-killers at seed+
Demand evidence
Revenue, retention, waitlist with conversion data, or signed LOIs. Something that proves people want this.
⚑ Tier-1 killer at seed+ if < 30
50
Tier 2 — Differentiators
Growth velocity
Rate of growth over last 90 days. Investors fund momentum, not absolute size at early stages.
50
Retention quality
Are users staying? Are revenue cohorts holding up? Strong retention can offset weaker growth at early stages.
50
Unit economics direction
Improving, flat, or deteriorating as the company has grown. Direction matters more than absolute level at early stages.

Fundable Score / Dimension 04
Round Readiness

Even a strong company can fail to raise if the round itself is structured wrong. This dimension is about how the ask is framed — not the substance behind it.

Weight: 15% at pre-seed
Tier 1 — Deal-killers
Milestone clarity
Can the founder name the specific proof point this round gets them to — and why it changes the next-round story?
⚑ Tier-1 killer if < 30
50
Tier 2 — Differentiators
Stage-ask fit
Is the round size and valuation appropriate for the traction? Asking too high signals naivety. Asking too low signals fear.
50
Use of funds specificity
"Hire 2 sales, run 90 days of channel tests" beats "expand the team and grow." Specificity signals operational maturity.
50
Risk transparency
Does the founder name the top 2 risks and what they're doing about each? Hidden risks signal naivety; named risks signal sophistication.

Narrative Quality
